Hover the name of any lane (on the left of the timeline, or in the table) to read what it is and what to check.

Space play / pause · ← → 1 s (Shift 5 s) · , . previous / next beat · mouse wheel zooms · click the sound wave, the curves or the bar under them to jump there.

Move: drag a mark, or the edge of a stretch. Select it and Alt+← → moves it 10 ms.

Delete: click a mark or a stretch, then Del.

Add: double-click in a lane, or pick a lane (click its name) and press A to add at the white line, even while playing. Stretches: Shift+drag, or [ start and ] end at the white line.

The "1" of the bar: select a beat and press 1 (or 2, 3, 4): the beats after it are renumbered.

Half-beat: select a beat and press M to switch it between main beat and half-beat.

Colours: faint = one tool or weak, solid = two tools agree, white dot on top = placed or moved by you. Tick "checked whole" when a lane is right from start to end.

Ctrl+Z undo. "Back to the draft" throws away your changes in one lane.
