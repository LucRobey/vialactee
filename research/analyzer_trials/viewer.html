Tick "click" on one tracker at a time: the click is mixed into the audio, so it stays in sync even on Bluetooth headphones (the lamps can lag there). Red in the waveform is the bass band, where the kick drum sits. A white frame and "1" mark a downbeat. In the Combined lane, taller ticks are beats that more trackers agree on, and its lamp is brighter the harder the drums hit. Red-shaded stretches are marked NO BEAT (Beat This and BeatNet do not land together): the Combined lamp and click stay silent there. They are also marked in red along the bottom of the overview bar.
